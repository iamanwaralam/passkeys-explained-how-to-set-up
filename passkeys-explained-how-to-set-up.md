# Passkeys Explained: How to Set Up and Use Them

Passkeys are replacing passwords. Here's what they are and how to turn one on.

## What a passkey actually is
A passkey is a cryptographic key pair stored on your device. Instead of typing a password, you unlock it with your fingerprint, face, or PIN — and it signs you in. Nothing to steal, phish, or reuse.

## How to set one up
1. Go to your account's Security settings (Google, Microsoft, Apple all support passkeys)
2. Find "Passkeys" or "Sign in with a passkey" and choose Add/Create
3. Confirm with fingerprint, face unlock, or PIN
4. Done — next sign-in offers the passkey instead of a password
